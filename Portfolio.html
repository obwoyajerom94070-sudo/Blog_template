About Me

I am Obwoya Jerom Benson, PE, a Lead Reservoir Engineer with deep expertise in optimizing mature assets and greenfield developments. My engineering workflow integrates fluid phase behavior (PVT), pressure diagnostics, and 3D reservoir simulation models to de-risk capital investments and quantify reserve uncertainty.

Throughout my career, I have successfully executed full-field development plans (FDP), waterflood voidage replacement strategies, and production forecasts that directly inform executive board decisions.

My engineering philosophy centers on rigorous physics-based modeling backed by automated data workflows. By pairing traditional material balance and numerical simulation with custom Python scripts and machine learning algorithms, I help operations teams optimize well spacing, completion designs, and injection strategies with reduced uncertainty.
